⚠️ DISCLAIMER — READ BEFORE USE

This application is a digital aid only. It interpolates from digitised AFM charts and small errors can accumulate — particularly at close performance margins. Computed values may differ from a careful hand-trace of the paper AFM.

At tight margins, always verify with a hand trace of the applicable AFM figure. This tool does not replace the AFM, crew judgement, or authorised performance documentation.

Chart data sourced from AFM EU02X503A Issue 3 Rev.12 (Vol 1) and FRC EU02X510A Issue 2 with AL 3.

Use of this application is at the operator's own risk. The developer accepts no liability for decisions made on the basis of its output.
